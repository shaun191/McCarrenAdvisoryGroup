An advocate, not a broker

We're not business brokers. Good ones help get a business sold — but they're paid on commission, so preparing your company to command its strongest value isn't always where that model points. We work only for you — protecting the business and guiding you through whatever comes next: a future sale, private equity, an ESOP, or handing it to the next generation, with seasoned attorneys and accountants brought in when you need them. That can include strengthening the core first — weighing whether to divest a non-core division or deploy capital into a well-chosen acquisition.
